# Welcome to my new blog

Welcome to my completely static, zero-server blog! 

## Features

- **Static first**: Everything is served as plain HTML/JS/CSS.
- **Markdown powered**: Just drop `.md` files in the `public/posts` directory.
- **Dark mode built-in**: A clean, elegant reading experience for any lighting condition.
- **Like button**: Supported by Firebase Firestore. (Wait, I thought there was no server? Well, serverless!)
